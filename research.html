Research

My research is primarily concerned with graph theory and combinatorics. I am especially interested in random graphs (random lifts of graphs), graph dimension (as a dimension of its incidence poset) and algorithms on graphs.
I am also interested in game theory, nonrepetitive words, probabilistic methods, and heuristics.
